# What the current HR-MAPPO study demonstrated — and did not demonstrate

**Scope:** source-only standalone extraction of the 12-active / 2-reserve HR-MAPPO research implementation. The values below are taken from the **previously completed four-phase recovery study** (three independent training seeds per treatment, 160 PPO updates each) and the subsequent **paired causal diagnostic**; none are generated by the standalone extraction.

## Evidence from the nine-run study

| Treatment | Mean development physical team return | Policy-conditioned stress recovery rate (per-seed average) | Exploratory seeds passing original recovery gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Pure shared team reward** | **986.11** | **53.3%** | **2/3** |
| Shared reward + Shapley service credit | 921.60 | 40.0% | 1/3 |
| Shared reward + Shapley + overstaff cost | 950.21 | 38.9% | 0/3 |

**Interpret carefully:** the policy-conditioned stress cuts differ between policies. They are **not** matched episode return comparisons. Three independent training seeds per treatment are exploratory, not a publication-grade uncertainty estimate.

- The two best Pure Team Reward policies recorded **4/5** physical restorations each under tailored cuts; the third recorded **0/5**.
- In ordinary fixed-fault development episodes, Pure Team Reward recorded **0 active restorations across 11 actual outages**. Active repair remains difficult in realistic mixed scenarios.
- Shapley + overstaff did **not** reliably reduce surplus staffing in the three-run study. More elaborate reward shaping was not supported as better.
- Training can produce useful physical service and recurring coalition formation. It does **not**, by itself, establish learned failure-responsive strategy.

## Crucial causal counterfactual — NO-GO for a recovery claim

Three Pure Team Reward checkpoints (training seeds 101, 202, 303) were evaluated on **15 matched fault/no-fault scenarios** with identical pre-fault action histories. A policy-conditioned service cut was applied at step 50. The test compared surviving rovers' decentralized observations, action probabilities and **chosen tasks**, not just resumption of physical service.

| Measure | Count |
|---|---:|
| Stress cases with actual service drop | 15/15 |
| Physical service restorations | 8/15 |
| Cases with changed local survivor observations | 15/15 |
| Cases with any fault-caused changed task choice | 1/15 |
| **Fault-caused new assignment to the disrupted POI** | **0/15** |

All **8** physical restorations occurred with surviving agents following choices they would also have made with **no fault**. Thus the central research claim — *the Actor learns targeted reallocation because it observes teammate failure* — **is not supported**. A constructive privileged repair witness verifies physical feasibility in the diagnostic, but is never input to the Actor or used as a fair decentralized baseline.

## Supported engineering capabilities

- Shared MLP-GRU, masked categorical POI/IDLE head, recurrent centralized team-value critic (CTDE).
- Per-agent observation and local-communication constraints; no runtime centralized allocator.
- Asynchronous decisions and macro-step discounting, train/evaluate checkpoint and RNG resume path.
- Explicit coupled physical service rules requiring intention + radius + compatible skill.
- Reproducible randomized POI layouts, skill constraints, windows, faults and replacement events.
- Strong same-information heuristic comparators, separate policy-conditioned stress diagnostics.

## Not yet demonstrated / constraints

- Robust **causal** recovery: **NO**; policy choices were almost invariant to failure.
- A consistent advantage over the best local non-RL selector across independent seeds and blind held-out scenarios: **NO**.
- Benefits of GRU versus attention, or of event-triggered decisions versus synchronous: **not established**.
- D++ or full MARL counterfactual credit: **not implemented**. Present `shapley_service` is exact symmetric per-tick threshold-game service credit, **not D++**.
- CUDA performance optimization of physical simulation: **not done**; physical environment remains CPU/NumPy.
- Future controlled tiny recovery-learning benchmark and any modifications motivated by it: **not included**. The current branch is the freeze point for later experiments.

## Replication and publication cautions

Do not include this repository's training logs, illustrative tests, checkpoint selection or previous studies as new paper results unless the **actual checkpoint hashes, experiment configs, run manifests, seed lists, and data files** are archived separately and attributed to their real runs. Development seeds can be used for tuning; held-out seeds should only be consumed under a frozen, predetermined protocol.

The old guided-movement DART-MARL results were **secondary privileged-information comparisons** and are intentionally not part of this standalone HR-MAPPO branch.
